How The Aviary Works

📤 For the Sender (You):

When you release your bird, it embarks on a physical journey across the globe based on real-world geographic distance. Your bird will fly in real-time, taking hours or even days depending on its base speed, weather, and rest stops.

⚠️ The Risk of the Journey:

Every bird has a "Risk Rate" which dictates its chance of crashing due to exhaustion, storms, or predators. If your bird crashes, it fails its delivery.

  • If you checked "Burn": The letter is instantly destroyed to protect your privacy. It is gone forever.
  • If you left "Burn" unchecked: Your crashed bird falls to the public World Map. It now relies on the Yatramore community. Other strangers can discover your crashed bird and tap "Rescue". If exactly 2 rescuers push your bird, it will recover and complete its journey! (Don't worry, rescuers can only read a tiny 80-character snippet of your smudged letter—your privacy is safe).
📥 For the Receiver (Your Match):
  • Building Anticipation: Your match will actually see the incoming bird flying towards them in their "My Perch" inbox! They can track its flight progress in real-time, building anticipation, but they cannot read the letter until it safely lands.
  • A Flawless Flight: If the bird survives the journey without crashing, they will receive your letter in pristine, perfect condition.
  • A Rescued Flight: If your bird crashed and was saved by 2 community rescuers, the letter will arrive damaged. About 35% of your words will be washed away or torn, replaced with mysterious smudges like [weather-stained]. Your match will receive a beautifully fragmented message that survived against all odds!

Community Rescue

When a bird fails to complete its journey due to exhaustion or bad weather, it crashes. What happens next depends on your choice:

World Map (Unchecked)

Your crashed bird will appear on the public World Map. Any other user on Yatramore can discover it and tap "Rescue" to help it along. If enough kind strangers push your bird, it will complete its journey and deliver your letter safely to your match!

Note: Surviving a crash is tough! About 35% of the words in your letter will be randomly destroyed and replaced with atmospheric smudges like [weather-stained]. Rescuers will be able to see a short, 80-character snippet of this smudged letter on the map to give them a glimpse of the mysterious message they are saving!

🔥 Burn Letter (Checked)

If privacy is your top concern, check this box. Upon crashing, your letter will instantly ignite and destroy itself. It will never be seen on the World Map, but this also means it can never be rescued.
